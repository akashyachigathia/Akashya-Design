I’m Akashya, a designer based in Birmingham with over six years of experience across graphic, web, and UI design. I enjoy making complex ideas easier to understand and turning them into digital experiences that feel clear and intuitive. These days, I’m building on that foundation with front-end development, bringing more of what I design to life through code.


Originally from Gurgaon, India, I moved to the UK to study for a Master’s in User Experience Design. My research into web accessibility shaped how I think about design: who can use it, where they might struggle, and what I can do to make it work better for them.


Outside of design, I’m usually exploring new tech and AI tools, gaming on my PC, watching sci-fi, or heading somewhere new with my camera. I also make time for the gym, a good book, and the occasional break from screens—although my idea of a break sometimes involves a different screen.
